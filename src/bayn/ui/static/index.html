        Train the triple-blink detector

        You'll be prompted to blink, nod, turn your head, look sideways and talk. Every prompt labels the EEG, and a classifier learns to tell a deliberate triple blink from everything else, double blinks included. Sessions are saved, so each new one makes the detector better.

        Standard: clean examples of each gesture. Noisy: triple blinks while talking, looking around and moving, to test robustness. Boundaries: soft / fast / slow triple blinks and the closest look-alikes (four blinks, squeezing, nodding three times...), to teach the edges. Do a standard one first.
